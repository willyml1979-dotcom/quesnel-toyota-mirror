Introducing the 2026 Toyota Tacoma 4X4 TRD Sport+, a rugged and refined midsize truck designed to deliver capability, technology, and everyday comfort in one confident package. Finished in striking Celestial Silver Metallic, this Double Cab AT stands out with a bold TRD Front Grille, hood scoop, body-coloured overfenders, black mud guards, LED fog lamps, and a sporty stance enhanced by its 18-inch wheels wrapped in 265/65 R18 all-season tires. The 6-foot bed adds versatility for work or weekend adventures, while the heavy-duty tow hitch receiver and trailer brake controller provide the confidence needed for hauling and towing duties. Under the hood, the Tacoma is powered by a responsive 2.4L 4-cylinder turbocharged gas engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and Four Wheel Drive, delivering strong performance with the added control of part-time 4WD.

The sport-tuned suspension, Drive Mode Select, rear coil springs, and active grille shutters contribute to a balanced driving experience that feels composed on pavement and capable off the beaten path. Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 is also on board, offering advanced driver assistance features such as Pre-Collision System, Lane Tracing Assist, Road Sign Assist, Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross Traffic Alert, Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, and Proactive Driving Assist. Inside, the TRD Sport+ blends durability and premium amenities with fabric seats, heated front seats, 8-way power adjustable driver and passenger seats, lumbar support, and a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel. Technology is thoughtfully integrated with wireless Apple CarPlay, wireless Android Auto, a 10-speaker JBL audio system with portable Bluetooth speaker, wireless charger, digital key with card key, and a 12.3-inch display.

Additional conveniences include a power slide/tilt moonroof, power sliding rear window, automatic climate control, and multiple USB-C and 12V outlets. With its commanding design, advanced safety, and everyday utility, the 2026 Tacoma TRD Sport+ is built for drivers who want a truck that is as capable as it is well equipped.
