Experience refined versatility in the 2026 Toyota Sienna Hybrid XSE AWD 7-Pass, a stylish family vehicle engineered to deliver efficiency, comfort, and confidence in equal measure. Powered by a 2.5-litre 4-cylinder DOHC 16-valve hybrid engine with Toyota Hybrid System (THS), Direct Injection D4-S, VVT-i, and VVT-iE, this model pairs smooth performance with impressive fuel economy. Its electronically controlled continuously variable transmission and electronic on-demand all-wheel drive help provide composed handling in changing road conditions, while sport-tuned suspension and bold XSE styling create a more athletic presence. Inside, the Sienna Hybrid XSE offers a premium, driver-focused cabin designed for modern life. Seven-passenger seating includes second-row long-slide captain’s chairs and a foldable 60/40 third row for flexible cargo and passenger arrangements. Heated front seats, a 4-way power lumbar driver seat, 8-way power adjustable driver seat, SofTex sport seating, and a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel enhance everyday comfort.

Four-zone independent automatic climate control keeps every row comfortable, while amenities such as a power slide/tilt moonroof, wireless charger, seven USB charging ports, and a 12V accessory connector add convenience throughout the cabin. Technology is equally advanced, highlighted by a 12.3-inch Toyota Multimedia system with Service and Safety Connect, Android Auto, Apple CarPlay, Bluetooth capability, SiriusXM satellite radio, and a 12-speaker JBL audio system. Safety is comprehensive, with Toyota Safety Sense features including Pre-Collision System with pedestrian and bicycle detection, Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist and Road Edge Detection, Lane Tracing Assist, Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross-Traffic Alert, and ten airbags. Additional features such as power sliding doors with kick sensor, power back door, premium LED headlamps, black roof rails, sport grille, and rear spoiler complete a highly equipped hybrid SUV built for families who value efficiency, sophistication, and capability.
