Midnight Black Metallic

Introducing the 2026 Toyota Sienna Hybrid LE, a refined and highly efficient family vehicle finished in striking Midnight Black Metallic. Designed with an 8-passenger layout, this versatile model combines the practicality of a minivan with the confident presence and elevated styling often associated with modern SUVs. Its sleek profile is complemented by black roof rails, body-coloured door handles, a rear spoiler, LED rear combination lamps, and a bold, contemporary stance that looks premium from every angle. Power comes from a 2.5-litre 4-cylinder DOHC 16-valve hybrid system paired with Toyota Hybrid System technology and an electronically controlled continuously variable transmission. Front-wheel drive delivers smooth, responsive performance, while impressive fuel efficiency helps keep everyday travel economical, with city and highway ratings both listed at 6.6 KM.

Multiple drive modes, including Eco, Normal, and Sport, allow you to tailor the driving experience to changing conditions. Inside, the Sienna Hybrid LE is thoughtfully equipped for comfort and convenience. Heated front seats, an 8-way power adjustable driver’s seat, a 4-way manual passenger seat, and 3-zone automatic climate control create a welcoming cabin for all occupants. The second-row sunshades, power dual sliding rear doors, and 3rd-row foldable 60/40 seats make loading passengers and cargo simple and flexible. Technology is equally impressive, with Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, a wireless charger, 7 USB charging ports, Bluetooth capability, SiriusXM satellite radio, and 8 speakers. Safety and driver assistance are standout strengths. Features such as Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross Traffic Alert, Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Lane Tracing Assist, Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist, Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian and Bicycle Detection, and a backup camera provide confidence on every journey.

With 10 airbags, Vehicle Stability Control, Anti-Lock Brakes, and Toyota’s Star Safety System, the 2026 Sienna Hybrid LE offers a secure, efficient, and family-ready driving experience.
