The 2026 Toyota Tundra Double Cab SR 4x4 is a full-size pickup engineered to deliver strong capability, modern technology, and everyday practicality in a bold Magnetic Metallic Gray finish. Powered by a twin-turbo 3.4L I-FORCE V6 engine producing 358 horsepower, this gas-powered truck pairs its robust output with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission and part-time 4WDemand four-wheel drive for confident performance on-road and off. With a Class IV tow rating, trailer sway control, trailer wiring harness, trailer hitch, and a rear bumper step, it is well prepared for hauling, towing, and weekend work duties. Inside, the Double Cab cabin offers durable cloth seating, a split-bench rear seat, 60-40 folding rear seats, and thoughtful storage throughout, including rear seatback pockets, door bins, and a covered floor console.

Comfort and convenience features include automatic climate control, cruise control with steering wheel-mounted controls, push-button start, keyless entry, remote start, and power windows and locks. Technology is equally well represented with an 8-inch touchscreen, integrated navigation with voice activation, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto wireless mirroring, Bluetooth connectivity, SiriusXM trial subscription, smart device access, and built-in virtual assistant support. Safety and driver assistance are comprehensive, with 8 airbags, Pre-Collision System with left turn assist, Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist, Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Vehicle Sway Warning, pedestrian impact prevention, rearview camera, blind-spot-free confidence from wide visibility, and Vehicle Stability Control with ABS and brake assist. LED headlights, LED daytime running lights, automatic high beams, heated mirrors, and deep-tinted glass add both visibility and refinement. Riding on 18-inch silver styled steel wheels and all-season tires, the 2026 Tundra combines rugged styling with practical utility.

It is a capable, well-equipped truck built for drivers who want proven Toyota durability, modern connectivity, and strong towing strength in a versatile 4x4 package.
