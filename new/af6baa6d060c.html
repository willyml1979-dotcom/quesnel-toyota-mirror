Under the hood, it is powered by a 2.4-liter 4-cylinder turbocharged gasoline engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission and part-time four-wheel drive. This setup provides responsive power delivery and added traction for challenging road conditions, off-pavement driving, and changing weather. The 6-foot bed adds impressive cargo versatility, while features such as bed rail adjustable tie-down cleats, cargo bed tie-downs, black mud guards, and a passenger-side front recovery hook enhance its utility and readiness for demanding tasks.
