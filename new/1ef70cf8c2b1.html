The 2027 Toyota Land Cruiser is a rugged yet refined SUV engineered to deliver exceptional capability, advanced technology, and everyday comfort in a distinctive Heritage Blue with Light exterior finish. Built on a proven Four Wheel Drive hybrid platform, it is powered by Toyota’s i-FORCE MAX 2.4L Turbo L4 Hybrid engine, pairing 2.4 liters and 4 cylinders with an 8-speed automatic transmission for confident performance across highways, trails, and challenging weather conditions. With estimated fuel efficiency of 10.7 KM in the city and 10.1 KM on the highway, it balances strength with practicality. Designed for adventure, the Land Cruiser features Full-Time 4WD, Crawl Control, Multi-Terrain Select, Downhill Assist Control, a locking rear differential, front stabilizer disconnect mechanism, and an engine skid plate for enhanced off-road confidence.

Its independent double wishbone front suspension and 4-link rear suspension with coil springs support a composed ride, while 265/60R20 tires, illuminated running boards, mudguards, bridge-type roof rails, tow hitch, and full-size spare tire underscore its readiness for demanding terrain. Inside, the cabin blends premium materials and advanced comfort. Leather seats, heated and ventilated front seats, heated and ventilated rear seats, an 8-way power adjustable driver seat with lumbar support, an 8-way power adjustable passenger seat, heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, power tilt and telescopic steering wheel, and driver seat memory create a tailored driving experience. Convenience features include a power back door, smart key with push-button start, digital key 2.0, remote connect trial, center console cooler box, cargo area mat, carpet floor mats, and a power slide/tilt moonroof. Technology is equally impressive, highlighted by a 14-speaker JBL audio system, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, a head-up display, digital display rearview mirror, Toyota Assistant, HD Radio, SiriusXM, Bluetooth, and six USB-C charging ports.

Safety is comprehensive with Toyota Safety Sense features such as Pre-Collision System, Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Lane Tracing Assist, Blind Spot Monitor with Rear Cross Traffic Alert, Traffic Jam Assist, Road Sign Assist, and a total of nine airbags. This Land Cruiser delivers premium capability with unmistakable presence.
