The 2026 Toyota Tacoma Hybrid 4X4 Double Cab is a highly capable, technology-rich midsize truck engineered for drivers who want rugged performance without sacrificing refinement or efficiency. Powered by Toyota’s I-FORCE MAX 2.4L turbocharged hybrid four-cylinder engine, it delivers 278 horsepower and pairs with an 8-speed automatic transmission and part-time Four Wheel Drive for confident traction on and off the pavement. This Tacoma is equally prepared for demanding work and weekend adventure, with a Class IV tow rating, trailer brake controller, trailer wiring harness, trailer sway control, a trailer hitch, and Toyota’s Straight Path Assist and reverse assist features for easier trailer maneuvering. Inside, the Double Cab offers a premium and functional cabin with SofTex leatherette seating, heated and ventilated front seats, a heated steering wheel, dual-zone climate control, and power-adjustable driver and passenger seats with lumbar support.

The cabin is enhanced by a 14-inch primary touchscreen, 12.3-inch gauge cluster display, head-up display, JBL premium audio with 9 speakers, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Qi wireless charging, and multiple USB ports. The digital rearview mirror, panoramic view monitor, multi-terrain monitor, and drive mode selection further elevate everyday usability and off-road confidence. Safety and driver assistance are comprehensive, including Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 with pre-collision mitigation, lane tracing assist, blind spot monitor, rear cross traffic alert, adaptive cruise control, road sign assist, and parking sensors. Off-road hardware such as crawl control, selectable terrain modes, rear locking differential, skid plates, all-terrain tires, and off-road suspension reinforce its adventurous character. Finished with bronze aluminum wheels, power running boards, a power tailgate, and a full suite of modern conveniences, the 2026 Tacoma Hybrid blends toughness, innovation, and premium comfort in one versatile truck.
